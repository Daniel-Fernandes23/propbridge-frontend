Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is PropBridge really free to list?+
Yes — 100% free to list. You only pay our 2.5% platform fee if your property sells through a buyer we connected you with. No upfront costs, no monthly fees, no hidden charges.
How fast can I really close?+
Most cash buyers close in 7–21 days because they pay cash — no mortgage approval, no bank delays, no financing contingencies holding things up.
Do I need to make repairs before listing?+
No. Our contractor buyers specialize in as-is purchases. They factor repair costs into their offers. You don't need to clean, stage, or fix anything.
How is PropBridge different from Zillow?+
Zillow is built for retail buyers using mortgages who need 60–90 days and require repairs. PropBridge is built for cash contractors who close in 7–21 days and buy as-is. We also cost half as much as a traditional agent.
What markets does PropBridge cover?+
PropBridge is currently focused on 10+ major markets nationwide with active cash buyer networks in both cities. Expanding to additional markets throughout 2025.
How does PropBridge collect its fee?+
The 2.5% platform fee is collected at closing via our secure Stripe payment system — handled automatically. No invoicing, no chasing payments.
