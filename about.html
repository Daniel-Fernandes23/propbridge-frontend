Our Mission

Why We
Exist

PropBridge exists to eliminate the inefficiency, cost, and opacity of traditional residential real estate transactions — connecting sellers and buyers directly, without the friction that has defined the industry for decades.

The average home seller pays 5–6% of their sale price to agents who spend weeks arranging showings, negotiating with mortgage-dependent buyers, and navigating a process designed for a different era. The average buyer waits 60–90 days to close, often on a property that required significant repairs just to qualify for financing.

PropBridge is a different model entirely. Sellers list for free and receive direct offers from cash buyers within 48 hours. Buyers access off-market properties before they reach the MLS. Transactions close in 7–21 days. And the platform fee — 2.5%, paid only at closing — is a fraction of what traditional agents charge.

We are building the marketplace that real estate has always needed. One deal, one connection, one closing at a time.
