Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

Is PropBridge really free to list?+
Yes — listing your property on PropBridge is completely complimentary. You only pay our 2.5% platform fee if and when your property sells through a buyer introduced through our platform. There are no upfront costs, no monthly fees, and no hidden charges of any kind.
How does PropBridge compare to selling with an agent?+
Traditional agents typically charge 5–6% commission, require 60–90 days to close, and demand repairs and staging. PropBridge charges 2.5% at closing, connects you with cash buyers who close in 7–21 days, and requires absolutely no repairs or staging.
Do I need to repair or clean my property before listing?+
No. Our contractor buyers specialize in as-is acquisitions. They evaluate properties in their current condition and factor any renovation costs into their offers. You are not required to make any repairs, improvements, or even deep clean before listing.
How quickly can I realistically close?+
Most transactions on PropBridge close within 7–21 days from accepted offer. This is achievable because our buyers are cash investors — there is no mortgage approval process, no bank underwriting, and no financing contingencies that could delay closing.
How is the 2.5% platform fee collected?+
The platform fee is collected at closing through our secure payment system. It is processed simultaneously with the transaction — there is no invoicing, no manual transfers, and no chasing payments. You receive your net proceeds and PropBridge receives the platform fee at the same moment.
What markets does PropBridge operate in?+
PropBridge is currently active in Houston, Atlanta, Memphis, Dallas, Detroit, Cleveland, Jacksonville, Birmingham, Kansas City, and Philadelphia — with additional markets being added regularly. If your market is not listed, contact us directly as we may still be able to facilitate a connection.
